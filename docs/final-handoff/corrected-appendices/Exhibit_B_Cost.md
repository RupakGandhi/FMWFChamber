### Combined Ongoing Maintenance — Corporate Cup and Application Management Platform

Beginning **January 1, 2027**, the Client has selected annual combined maintenance for the existing Corporate Cup platform and the FMWF Chamber Application Management Platform at a total fee of **$3,000 per year**. The annual maintenance term renews each January 1 unless ended in accordance with the Agreement.

Combined maintenance provides prioritized support for both platforms, continued OptimizED access for support and administration, troubleshooting, configuration assistance, routine updates, and reasonable minor adjustments within the existing platform architecture. Materially new modules, major redesigns, third-party integrations, substantial data migrations, or other work outside the existing architecture require separate written scope and approval.

No separate OptimizED hosting, domain-registration, or SSL-certificate fee is included or charged under this Exhibit. If a new third-party provider cost becomes necessary for the approved production architecture, that cost must be disclosed to and approved by the Client before commitment.
