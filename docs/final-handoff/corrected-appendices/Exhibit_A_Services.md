# Exhibit A

## Services

The Contractor shall design, configure, develop, and deploy the **FMWF Chamber Application Management Platform**, a custom Chamber-branded platform for managing applications, nominations, event registrations, reviewer assignments, scoring, communications, reporting, and event-day operations. The Platform will initially be configured for **Leadership FMWF**, **The Awards / Chamber Choice Awards**, and **Military Appreciation Night**. The Platform will include public application forms that do not require applicant accounts; configurable form fields, required fields, conditional logic, deadlines, scoring criteria, review rules, and program settings; Chamber-managed administrator accounts; secure account-free reviewer links; multiple-reviewer assignment, reassignment, score editing, comparison, tabulation, and ranking; editable email templates and Chamber-controlled email configuration; applicant status tracking; annual archives; CSV, Excel, and printable PDF exports; and mobile-responsive administrator, applicant, reviewer, and event-day interfaces.

The Military Appreciation Night configuration will include manual ticket-allocation control; requested-ticket totals and configurable threshold alerts; participant and commander follow-up forms; guest, T-shirt size, name-pronunciation, rank, branch, and years-of-service information; accepted-participant check-in; Picked Up, Cancelled, No-Show, and Undo actions; optional future-eligibility flagging; and capacity-based waitlist management with administrator-controlled promotion. The Platform will not automatically approve ticket requests or allocate tickets on a first-come, first-served basis.

The Contractor will also provide a responsive **FMWF Chamber Apps Hub** that connects the Application Management Platform, the existing Corporate Cup platform, and future Chamber applications from a unified entry point. The permanent hosting architecture and Chamber-controlled domains or redirects will be configured in coordination with the Client’s designated developer, with ongoing administrator and technical access retained for the Client and OptimizED Strategic Solutions for contracted maintenance and support.

The Client may use the Platform’s **Add New Program** functionality to create additional programs, events, application cycles, nominations, registrations, or hiring/application workflows when those uses can be configured through the Platform’s existing supported form, scoring, review, status, communication, export, waitlist, and event-management structures. A new custom module, materially different workflow, external integration, or data model that cannot be configured through the existing structures is outside the initial implementation and will be handled through the ongoing maintenance process or a separately approved change in scope.

Upon delivery, the Contractor will provide initial configuration of the three named programs; secure initial administrator-account setup; production deployment based on the Chamber-approved demonstration at **https://fmwfapps-j4ebl3f4.manus.space**; administrator training; source code and technical handoff materials for the Client’s designated developer; a Guided Tour and Help Center; production testing; and a post-delivery review period through January 1, 2027 for feedback and corrections within the agreed scope. The Platform is delivered as a one-time implementation for year-over-year use, with the Client maintaining ownership of its instance of the software in accordance with the Agreement.
